About Selena

Hi, I'm Selena. If you've seen a reel of a girl dancing like the rent is due and the lights just came on — that was probably me.

I live in rehearsals. Not the glamorous kind you see in montages — the real kind, with blistered feet, a bag that weighs more than I do, and a playlist that has opinions. I learned early that the stage doesn't care how you feel; it cares what you bring. So I bring everything.

Somewhere between the hundredth run-through and the first real applause, I started writing things down — what survives a rehearsal, what doesn't, what I'd tell my best friend before her first shoot. That's brutal truth: my digital diary. Bold opinions, tested-to-breaking honesty, and an occasional corner from my dashing cousin on how the other half lives.

And then there's Team Selena. I founded it because talent shouldn't wait for permission. Aanya Rai joined my team, and she's electric — go see her world when you're done with mine. More girls are coming. This site, though, stays personal: my reels, my photos, my words.

Stick around. The kettle's on, the ring light's warm, and I've got stories.
