A Note on Men's Grooming (from my cousin's playbook)

Guy's Guide corner, part two — my cousin returns, because you asked. Lightly edited by me (again).

After the boxer-brief announcement, my inbox — well, Selena's inbox — filled with grooming questions. Fine. Here's the playbook. Five things that matter, three that don't, zero apologies.

What matters: the haircut schedule

A good cut has a four-week expiry date. Week five isn't 'growing it out', it's neglect with a story. Book the next appointment at the appointment. Future you is busy.

What matters: the eyebrows

I'm not saying sculpt them. I'm saying the unibrow is a choice and it's choosing poorly. A ten-second tidy between the brows, once a week. That's the whole tutorial.

What matters: the nails and the nose

Trimmed nails, clean, no exceptions — people notice hands more than you think. And the nose hair situation: one trimmer, thirty seconds, dignity preserved. Moving on.

What matters: the scent

One signature scent, applied where a hug lands — not where the elevator lands. If people smell you before they see you, that's not a compliment, that's a weather event.

What matters: the skin basics

Cleanser, moisturizer, sunscreen. Three products. Men will research a fantasy football trade for six hours but won't spend ninety seconds on their face. Priorities, gentlemen.

What doesn't matter

The twelve-step routine, the jade roller, and whatever serum costs more than your shoes. Consistency with three basics beats chaos with twelve. — C.
